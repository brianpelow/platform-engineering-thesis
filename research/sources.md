# Research Sources

Supporting data cited in the thesis.

## AI ROI and failure rates

- Gartner (April 2026): Only 28% of AI I&O use cases meet ROI expectations. 57% of failures attributed to expecting too much, too fast.
- Grant Thornton Banking Insights 2026: 32% of banks report revenue growth from AI. 36% report cost reduction. Half cite governance barriers as primary constraint.
- Forrester 2026: AI will face a reckoning. Fewer than 1/3 of decision-makers can tie AI value to financial growth.

## AI inference cost trends

- 2026 AI Investment Guide (Kavout): LLM inference costs declining approximately 10x annually, faster than PC compute during the microprocessor revolution.
- Deloitte Tech Trends 2026: AI infrastructure budgets set to triple by 2028. Inference has become the critical cost driver.

## Machine-readable regulations

- IJACSA Survey (October 2025): Machine-readable regulations enable automated compliance checks, real-time monitoring, and automatic report generation.
- FinregE: Machine-readable regulation libraries enabling cross-jurisdictional compliance analysis.
- BeInformed (December 2025): Compliance is no longer a checklist. It is a living system.
- PillarsX Programmable Compliance (2026): CLARITY Act and GENIUS Act mandate in-ledger rule enforcement. Agentic systems require machine-readable compliance rules at point of execution.

## Agentic SDLC

- McKinsey (February 2026): A single person managing 20-30 agents. Banks must provide comprehensive AI offerings or risk disruption.
- Backbase Banking Predictions 2026: Agentic AI and multi-agent orchestration reshaping the SDLC end to end.
- BDO Fintech 2026: Agentic AI must deliver financial outcomes that appear in the ledger, not just productivity reports.

## Regulatory direction

- FDIC (March 2026): Supervision 360 — regulators building machine-readable supervisory processes.
- FinTech Global (April 2026): Machine-readable regulations changing regulator-institution relationships.
- Fintech trends 2026: AI must be explainable, infrastructure must be resilient, compliance must be automated.
